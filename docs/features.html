System Features & Modules

Explore how CLINICSYNC unites patients, receptionists, and dentists on an automated scheduling platform.

Patient Experience

Online Booking & Patient Portal

Patients can register an account, manage their verified mobile number, and submit appointment requests with their preferred dentist, date, and time slot.

  • 24/7 web booking without phone hold times
  • Live status tracking: Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled
  • Reschedule and cancellation requests with audit logging
  • View full history of SMS notifications received
Live Slot Availability Engine

Dynamically queries the doctor's weekly shift and booked slots to present only available slots.

09:00 AM (Available) 09:30 AM (Available) 10:00 AM (Booked) 10:30 AM (Available)
Clinic Administration

Staff Triage & Schedule Management

Receptionists maintain total visibility over the clinic calendar, resolving appointment requests, managing dentist availability, and reviewing delivery logs.

  • Single-click Confirm or Reject with custom feedback
  • Concurrency protection preventing doctor double-booking
  • Daily and weekly clinic calendar agenda
  • Patient directory and communication logs
Concurrency Lock Protection

Database transaction isolation verifies slot uniqueness at write-time, preventing race conditions when multiple patients request identical slots.

Messaging Infrastructure

Automated SMS Confirmation & Reminders

Never lose a patient to a forgotten appointment. CLINICSYNC dispatches automated SMS alerts immediately upon confirmation and triggers 24-hour reminder broadcasts.

  • Instant confirmation SMS upon staff approval
  • Scheduled 24-hour appointment reminder cron job
  • Pluggable gateway drivers (Simulated, Twilio, Semaphore)
  • Delivery status tracking with automatic retry dispatch

Practitioner Portal

Dedicated Dentist Schedule View

Dentists access a streamlined, distraction-free agenda showing their daily appointments, patient contact information, and appointment completion tools.

  • Personalized day/week agenda
  • Mark appointments as "Completed" upon treatment
  • Clinical notes and consultation remarks
