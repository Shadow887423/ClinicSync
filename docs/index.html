Streamlined Workflow

How CLINICSYNC Works

A clear, validated appointment lifecycle built for patients, administrative staff, and dentists.

1. Patient Requests Slot

The patient chooses a dentist, preferred service, date, and available time slot online 24/7.

2. Staff Verification

Clinic staff review the request, verify doctor availability, and confirm or modify the schedule.

3. Automated SMS Sent

The patient instantly receives a branded text message confirmation with date, time, and assigned dentist.

4. 24h SMS Reminder

Automated queue workers dispatch reminder texts 24 hours prior to the appointment to reduce no-shows.
